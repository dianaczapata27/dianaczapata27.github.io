Legados Invisibles

© 2025 - Diana C. Zapata Zuluaga - FACom

Rosalind Franklin

(1920-1958)
British chemist recognized today for her discovery of the helical structure of DNA through X-ray diffraction.

Jeanne Baret

(1740-1807)
French botanist documented as the first woman to carry out a botanical expedition. She catalogued various species of the genus Bougainvillea .

She reflects how women had to change their identity by dressing as men in order to be able to study.

Maryam Mirzakhani

(1977-2017)
Iranian mathematician, first woman to win the Fields Medal for her work in hyperbolic and symplectic geometry.

To learn more about her life and work, you can watch the documentary:
Secrets of the Surface: The Mathematical Vision of Maryam Mirzakhani .

Ana María Rey

(1977- )
Colombian physicist, noted for her work in atomic, molecular, and optical physics, ultracold atoms, and quantum information.

Ángela Restrepo

(1931-2022)
Colombian microbiologist. She was a professor at Universidad de Antioquia (Medellin, Colombia), with an extensive career in the investigation of diseases caused by fungi and their treatment.

Rebeca Gerschman

(1903-1986)
Argentine chemist, pioneer in the study of the production of free radicals and their influence on biological systems.

Doris Hinestroza

(1954-2019)
Colombian mathematician. She was a professor and researcher at Universidad del Valle (Cali, Colombia), recognized for her work on applied inverse problems.

Katherine Johnson

(1918-2020)
American astrophysicist, recognized for calculating the orbit for the first Moon landing and for being part of NASA's “human computers,” a group of African American women.

To learn more about her story, you can watch the film Hidden Figures .
